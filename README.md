# HQViT Data Efficiency Study

## Research Title
Investigating Data Efficiency and Generalisation in Hybrid Quantum-Classical Vision Transformers

## Research Objectives
- Compare hybrid quantum-classical and classical vision models.
- Evaluate performance using 10%, 25%, 50%, and 100% of the training data.
- Measure variability across random seeds.
- Analyse generalisation and computational trade-offs.
- Investigate whether hybrid models provide measurable benefits under controlled conditions.

## Experimental Principles
- Use identical data splits wherever possible.
- Keep evaluation metrics consistent across models.
- Record random seeds and hyperparameters.
- Report mean and standard deviation across repeated runs.
- Do not assume quantum advantage before examining results.
